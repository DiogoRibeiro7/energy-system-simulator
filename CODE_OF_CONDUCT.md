# Code of Conduct

## Our Standards

This project expects contributors to participate respectfully and constructively.
Examples of behavior that contributes to a productive environment include:

- Using welcoming and inclusive language.
- Respecting different viewpoints and experience levels.
- Giving and accepting constructive feedback.
- Focusing discussion on what is best for the project and its users.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, personal attacks, trolling, public or
private abuse, and publishing others' private information without permission.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove comments, issues, pull requests, or other
contributions that do not meet these standards. Maintainers may also restrict
participation when behavior is harmful to the project or its contributors.

Report concerns privately to the repository owner or another project maintainer.
Reports will be reviewed as promptly and discreetly as possible.
