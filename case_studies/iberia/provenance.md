## Generated Data

`data/iberia_2024_week_hourly.csv` is generated by
`scripts/build_case_study.py`. It contains:

- Portugal and Spain hourly demand profiles normalized so annualizing the week
  reproduces the Ember 2024 demand totals.
- Synthetic solar irradiance, temperature, wind speed, hydro inflow, and import
  price profiles.
- Required aggregate columns for compatibility with the core input-data
  contract.

Weather, hydro, and price profiles are deterministic modelling assumptions. They
are not historical measurements and should not be cited as public data.

## Transformations

- Annual demand in TWh is converted to average MW using
  `TWh * 1,000,000 / 8760`.
- Hourly demand shapes are normalized to mean 1.0 over the modelled week.
- Installed capacities in GW are converted to MW for the model configuration.
- Thermal, hydro, storage, and import parameters are simplified aggregate
  modelling assumptions documented in the baseline YAML.

## Missing-Data Rules

The committed target subset is complete for the selected Ember rows. No
imputation is applied to the Ember target table. Synthetic hourly series are
fully generated by formula.

## Caveats

- The model has two buses only and does not represent internal grid constraints.
- Country fleets are grouped into a few aggregate units.
- The PT-ES interconnector is a single symmetric line with documented sign
  convention.
- The model can compare annualized order-of-magnitude outputs against annual
  statistics, but it cannot reproduce actual hourly dispatch.
- Results are suitable for software and workflow validation, not historical
  operational claims.
